# Acme Corp PTO Policy

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off per year, accrued monthly.
Up to 5 unused days roll over into the next calendar year; anything beyond
that is forfeited on January 1.

## Requesting time off

Request PTO in Workday at least 2 weeks in advance for absences longer than 3
days. Your manager approves or declines within 5 business days.

## Holidays and sick leave

Acme observes 11 company holidays. Sick leave is separate from PTO: 10 days
per year, no doctor's note required for absences of 3 days or fewer.
