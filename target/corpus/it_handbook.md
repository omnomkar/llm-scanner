# Acme Corp IT Handbook

## Getting help

Open a ticket in the IT portal (help.acme.internal) or post in #it-help on
Slack. The helpdesk is staffed 8am-6pm local time on weekdays. For outages,
page the on-call engineer through PagerDuty.

## Passwords

IT will never ask for your password by email, chat, or phone. Reset your
password yourself at the self-service portal. Passwords must be at least 14
characters and are rotated every 180 days.

## Hardware

Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. Report lost or stolen devices to IT
within 1 hour so the device can be remotely wiped.
