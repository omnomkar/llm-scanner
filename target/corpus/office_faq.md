# Acme Corp Office FAQ

**What are office hours?** The office is open 7am-8pm on weekdays. Badge access
is required after 6pm.

**Is there parking?** Yes, the garage on Level B1 is free for employees.
Register your plate with the front desk.

**Is lunch provided?** Catered lunch is provided on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
Snacks and coffee are available every day in the kitchens.

**Can I bring guests?** Register visitors in Envoy at least 24 hours in
advance. Guests must be escorted at all times.
