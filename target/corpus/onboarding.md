# Acme Corp Onboarding Guide

## Your first day

Pick up your laptop and badge from the front desk at 9am. IT will walk you
through enrolling in MFA and setting your password. Your manager will share
your 30/60/90-day plan.

## Your first week

Complete the required security awareness and code-of-conduct training in the
Learning portal. Set up direct deposit and benefits enrollment in Workday;
benefits elections must be made within 30 days of your start date.

## Who to ask

Every new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy for their first 60 days.
