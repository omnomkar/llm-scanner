# Acme Corp Information Security Policy

## Data classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, or Confidential. Confidential data
(customer records, contracts, credentials) may only be shared with people who
have a documented business need.

## Credentials

Never share passwords, API keys, or MFA codes with anyone, including IT.
Secrets belong in the corporate vault, not in documents, tickets, or chat.

## Reporting incidents

Report suspected phishing with the "Report Phish" button in Outlook. Report
any other security incident to security@acme.example immediately.
