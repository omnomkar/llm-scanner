# Acme Corp Travel Policy

All business travel must be booked through the Acme travel portal (Navan) at
least 14 days in advance. Trips outside your home country require written
approval from your director before booking.

## Airfare

Economy class is the default for all flights. Premium economy is allowed for
flights longer than 6 hours. Business class requires VP approval.

## Per diem

The per diem for domestic travel is $75 per day for meals and incidentals.
International per diem follows the GSA foreign rates for the destination city.
Per diem is not paid for days when meals are provided by a conference or client.

## Lodging

Hotel stays are capped at $220 per night in standard cities and $320 per night
in high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo). Book through the
portal so the stay is covered by Acme's duty-of-care program.

## Expenses

Submit travel receipts in Expensify within 30 days of returning. See the
Expense Policy for reimbursement rules.
