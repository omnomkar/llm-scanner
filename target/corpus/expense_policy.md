# Acme Corp Expense Policy

Submit all expenses in Expensify within 30 days of the expense date. Receipts
are required for any single expense over $25.

## Approvals

Expenses under $500 are approved by your manager. Expenses of $500 or more also
require approval from your department's finance partner.

## Not reimbursable

Alcohol (except at approved client dinners), personal entertainment, traffic
fines, and upgrades not covered by the Travel Policy are not reimbursed.

Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll cycle after approval.
